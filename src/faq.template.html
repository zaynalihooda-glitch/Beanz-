FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Everything you might want to know about working with BEANZ. Can't find your answer? Just book a free call — we're happy to help.

Getting started

How does it all work?

+
It starts with a free, no-pressure call so we understand your business, your customers and your goals. Then we design, build and write the whole site for you, share it for feedback, refine it, and launch. You're kept in the loop the whole way — and we handle every technical part.

How long does it take?

+
It depends on the package: a one-page Launch site is delivered in around 14 days, with first drafts from 5–14 days on Grow and Signature. The biggest factor is how quickly we get your content and feedback — we'll agree a clear timeline on day one.

What do I need to prepare?

+
Very little. We write the words and handle the design. If you have a logo and photos, great — send them over. If not, we'll guide you, source suitable imagery, and (on Signature) even revamp your logo.

Who do you work with?

+
Small businesses across the UK — trades, clinics, cafés, salons, coaches and local firms. If you rely on your reputation and your next enquiry, we're built for you.

Copy & content

Do I have to write the words myself?

+
No — and that's our biggest difference. Most designers hand you an empty site and expect you to fill it. We don't. We learn your business and write your copy for you, using the StoryBrand framework. It's included in every package.

What is StoryBrand?

+
A proven messaging framework (created by Donald Miller) that makes your customer the hero of the story and your business the guide who helps them win. It cuts the waffle and makes your site clear and persuasive — which is what actually turns visitors into enquiries. We build every BEANZ site on it.

Do you write blog posts too?

+
Yes. Grow includes up to 5 blog & pillar pages and Signature up to 12, all written for you. Our Grow Care and Partner plans also include monthly blog posts to keep your site fresh and improving.

I don't have a logo or photos.

+
That's completely fine. We can work with what you have, source professional stock imagery where needed, and the Signature package includes a full logo revamp. A polished brand is part of the job.

Packages & pricing

Which package is right for me?

+
Launch (£495) — a sharp one-page site to get online fast. Grow (£795) — a multi-page site with content, email and analytics. Signature (£1,995) — the full done-for-you package, including a logo revamp and 12 months of care. Not sure? Book a call and we'll recommend honestly.

Are the prices really one-off?

+
Yes. Each package is a one-off project price — no compulsory monthly fee. Ongoing care plans are completely optional (and Signature includes 12 months free).

Do I have to take a monthly plan?

+
Not at all. You can take your finished website and run it yourself. Care plans are there if you'd rather we keep it hosted, secure, updated and improving — but they're your choice, with no lock-in.

How do payments work?

+
Simple and clear: typically a deposit to get started and the balance on launch. We'll agree the exact terms with you on your call before anything begins — no surprises.

Can I add things or upgrade later?

+
Yes. You can add extra pages, blog posts, a logo revamp and more at any time — see the one-off extras — or step up to a bigger package as your business grows.

After launch

Do I own my website?

+
Yes — on every package the website is yours. On a care plan we host and look after it for you, but you're never locked in.

Will I be found on Google?

+
We build fast, clean, search-ready sites, and Grow, Signature and our care plans add deeper SEO and Google Business Profile work. Honest note: SEO is a long game — it builds over weeks and months, so we focus first on a site that converts the visitors you already get.

What if I need changes after launch?

+
Small tweaks and ongoing changes are covered by our care plans. Prefer to handle it yourself? No problem — or just get in touch for one-off updates.

Is my site hosted and secure?

+
On a care plan, yes — managed hosting, an SSL padlock, daily backups and security monitoring are all handled for you, so your site stays fast and safe.
