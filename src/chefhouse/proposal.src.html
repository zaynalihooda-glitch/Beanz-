You have a 4.3-star restaurant and nowhere to send anyone.

What that costs you

Three things are happening right now that you can't see

1

Your reviews are doing work you never get paid for

A 4.3 average from 26 reviews is genuinely good, and it sits on a page you don't control, surrounded by competitors. On your own site those reviews are the first thing a hungry person reads — and the next thing they see is your phone number.

2

Three thousand followers have nowhere to land

Instagram gives you one link. Right now it goes nowhere, so every person who wants your menu, your hours or your address has to message you and wait for a reply — or give up and eat somewhere else.

3

The delivery apps own the customer, not you

Aggregators are useful and worth keeping. But every order placed through them costs you commission and gives you no way to reach that customer again. A site of your own is the one channel where the whole order is yours.
