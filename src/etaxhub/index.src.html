Questions people ask first

Do I have to use Xero?

It is included in every package and it is what we work in, so yes in practice. If you are on something else now, moving your records across is part of the job.

Am I tied into a contract?

No. The fee is monthly and you can stop. Your records are yours and you take them with you.

Does this cover my personal tax return?

Yes. Every package includes a director's self assessment, and Gold and Platinum cover two.

What if my bookkeeping is a mess?

Then you are in the right place, and you should say so on the call. It changes where we start, not whether we take it on.

Will I be dealing with you or with staff?

With Sadaf. It is a small practice on purpose, which is the whole reason things get spotted before the year end.

What does it cost beyond the monthly fee?

Nothing you have not agreed first. The fee is fixed, there is no hourly billing, and anything outside it gets quoted before it starts.
