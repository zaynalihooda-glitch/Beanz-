The name

Sip.ارتشي · irtashi · Arabic

That is what the name means. Not a word invented for a brand, and not an English word dressed up. It is the thing you came in to do.

ARCHI is an all day house. Coffee at seven in the morning for the people who start early, sandwiches through the middle of the day, and something sweet at one in the morning for the people who never quite go home.

What is on

Served from seven in the morning until two the following night, at both houses.

Specialty coffee

Brewed strong, the way the regulars ask for it. Hot chocolate when the coffee is not what you are after.

Sandwiches

Ciabatta, rye and club, built to order. The bread is the part worth turning up for.

Plates and bowls

Salads and bowls through the middle of the day, alongside fresh juices and smoothies.

Sweets

Something small at the end, for gatherings of every size.
